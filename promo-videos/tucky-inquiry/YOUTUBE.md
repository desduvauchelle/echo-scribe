## Description

It starts with one prompt you can't quite type. So you press a key and say it out loud.

Tucky is a free Mac app that turns your voice into clean text in any app: your AI assistant, email, Slack, code comments, notes. Talk, and the words land at your cursor without the "ums". Tell it "make this an email" and it rewrites in your voice.

Then it goes deeper:
• Everything you say lands in a searchable library
• Let it listen to your calls: live transcript, a guide that tracks what you've covered and suggests the next question, and the decisions and follow-ups already written when you hang up
• Weeks of meetings, notes and narrated screen recordings, sorted by project
• Ask it anything ("What did we decide on pricing with Acme?") and it answers with the source
• A daily recap: what happened, what matters, what's next

All of it lives on your Mac. No account. No cloud. No subscription. Free.
